Daily sets: 24 sets of 10

- One-variable equation fluency (8.EE.C.7)
- Multi-step equations with distribution (8.EE.C.7)
- Variables on both sides (8.EE.C.7)
- No-solution equations (8.EE.C.7)
- Infinitely-many-solution equations (8.EE.C.7)
- Equations with rational coefficients (8.EE.C.7)
- Modeling with one-variable equations (8.EE.C.7)
- Slope and rate of change (8.EE.B.5, 8.F.B.4)
- Linear vs. nonlinear functions (8.F.A.3)
- Build linear functions (8.F.B.4)
- Compare linear functions (8.F.A.2)
- Systems of linear equations (8.EE.C.8)
